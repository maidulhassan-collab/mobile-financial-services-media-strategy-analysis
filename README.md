## Project Overview

This project presents a strategic analysis of the *Mobile Financial Services (MFS) television advertising landscape* to understand how brands utilize media investment, campaign planning, and communication strategies to strengthen market positioning.

The study evaluates advertising effectiveness by analyzing:

- Share of Spend (SOS%)
- Share of Voice (SOV%)
- Media Efficiency Index
- Campaign Continuity
- Advertising Flighting Strategy
- Message Portfolio Analysis
- Daypart Advertising Strategy
- Competitive Positioning

The objective of this project is to transform advertising performance data into actionable marketing insights and strategic recommendations.

---

# Business Problem

The MFS industry is highly competitive, with multiple brands competing for consumer attention through television advertising.

However, higher advertising expenditure does not always result in higher visibility.

This project investigates:

- How efficiently brands convert media investment into consumer attention
- How market leaders maintain long-term visibility
- Why some competitors experience lower advertising efficiency
- Which communication areas represent future growth opportunities

---

# Project Objectives

The main objectives of this analysis are:

- Measure advertising efficiency using SOS and SOV comparison
- Identify successful media deployment strategies
- Analyze campaign continuity and seasonal advertising patterns
- Evaluate brand communication themes
- Identify strategic opportunities within the MFS category
- Provide actionable recommendations for future campaign planning

# Key Findings

## 1. Media Investment vs Visibility Efficiency

The analysis shows that advertising dominance is not only determined by spending volume.

Brands with efficient media planning can generate a higher Share of Voice compared with their Share of Spend.

![](https://github.com/maidulhassan-collab/mobile-financial-services-media-strategy-analysis/blob/main/Media%20files/SOS%20vs%20SOV.png)

### Key Insight:

> Strategic media buying and continuous presence can create stronger visibility efficiency than aggressive short-term spending.

---

# 2. Always-On Media Strategy

The analysis identifies two major advertising approaches:


![](https://github.com/maidulhassan-collab/mobile-financial-services-media-strategy-analysis/blob/main/Media%20files/always%20on.png)


### Continuous Presence Strategy

Characteristics:

- Regular media visibility
- Strong brand recall
- Long-term consumer engagement
- Protection against competitor activity

### Tactical Burst Strategy

Characteristics:

- Heavy investment during festive periods
- Short-term visibility increase
- Higher competition for premium airtime

### Insight:

Consistent media presence provides stronger long-term brand protection.

# Strategic Recommendations

## 1. Maintain Always-On Advertising Presence

Brands should maintain continuous communication instead of relying only on seasonal campaigns.

---

## 2. Focus on Ecosystem Value

Advertising should emphasize:

- Trust
- Convenience
- Financial ecosystem
- Digital lifestyle benefits

rather than competing only on price.

---

## 3. Optimize Advertisement Formats

Recommended approach:

### Long Format Ads

- Storytelling
- Emotional connection
- Product education


### Short Format Ads

- Brand reminder
